## What Study Hall does

- Class notebooks with sections and pages, in a layout like OneNote
- Notes written from photos of slides, textbook pages and handwritten work, **PDF, Word and PowerPoint files**, and **recorded lectures and voice notes** (with transcripts)
- **Ask the AI about your notes**: highlight anything and a ✨ Ask button appears; ask for more detail, derivations, examples or a quick quiz, with follow-ups. Answers are checked against Wikipedia (or a web search with a paid key) and show their sources; **Look it up** searches the web yourself
- **Dictation**, typing, fonts, highlights, comments, and pen/stylus drawing with five pen types; pictures and AI-redrawn diagrams you can move, resize and annotate
- **Sharing**: invite classmates to edit or view a notebook (changes sync live), or make a view-only link that opens in any browser
- **Export** to Word, Google Docs, PowerPoint, Google Slides, Notion, Evernote, Obsidian and PDF
- **Automatic backups to iCloud Drive** (or any folder) on Windows and Mac; one-tap backups on iPad and Android
- Flashcards generated from your notes, typed by hand, or imported from Quizlet; export back to Quizlet; Flashcards, Learn, Write and Test modes
- Practice tests and exam study guides written from a class's notes
- **Automatic updates** from this version on (Windows, Android, iPad; the Mac app tells you when a new version is out)
- A guided tour on launch, a feature guide and a searchable FAQ

AI features need a free Google Gemini key (or Claude, or Ollama). See **Help → FAQ** in the app.
